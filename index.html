Program Infrastructure
The 24-Week Participant Journey
Every participant moves through three phases. No phase is skipped. Every step is tracked and every participant has a named human responsible for their progress.
1
Intake and Orientation
Weeks 1–2
  • Intake survey — barriers, device access, career interest assessed
  • AI readiness scoring and credential track recommendation AI
  • Mentor matched within 48 hours — industry, goal, schedule aligned AI-matched
  • Device stipend or loaner issued — technology access guaranteed Day 1
2
Credential Track
Weeks 3–16 — Core Program
  • Week 3: 2-hour AI literacy workshop — study tools, career research, resume prep AI
  • Chosen credential: Healthcare, Technology, or Business and Entrepreneurship
  • Weekly mentor check-ins with automated engagement nudges AI
  • Daily job alerts and career coaching through Career Resources Aria
  • Credential earned: Aria Resume Agent builds employer-ready resume in under 5 minutes Aria
3
Pathway Activation and Placement
Weeks 17–24
  • Personalized career map — NJ salary ranges, next credential, career ladder
  • Warm introductions to named employer partners in participant's chosen track
  • Registered apprenticeship or direct hire within 90 days of credential completion
  • All outcomes tracked — credential, employer contact, offer, apprenticeship start Dashboard
+
Post-Program Community and Support
Months 6 · 12 · 18 · 24
  • 90 days of continued mentor support and career coaching post-placement
  • Monthly Saturday sessions — professional development, financial literacy, wellness
  • Retention tracked at 6, 12, 18, and 24 months
  • Alumni become mentors — the community grows the program organically
